HOW TO PLAY

Jujutsu Chess — Tactical Ritual
⚔ The Basics

Jujutsu Chess is a 1-vs-1 chess variant where each side wields cursed techniques alongside standard chess moves. You play as White against an AI opponent.

Win by checkmating the enemy King — or outlasting them with superior technique use. Standard chess rules apply: legal moves, check, checkmate, en passant, castling, and pawn promotion.

⚡ Cursed Energy (CE)

Both sides start with a fixed CE pool. CE does not regenerate passively — it is a finite resource you must spend wisely. Skills cost CE to activate — the cost is shown on each skill button.

  • Your CE bar is shown in blue; the enemy's in red.
  • Your CE pool starts at the highest ELO you have defeated (minimum 300). Beating harder bots increases your pool permanently.
  • Certain vows (Hunger, Divine, Sacrifice) modify your starting CE.
  • Six Eyes passive halves all skill costs for its holder.
  • Black Flash — any capture has a 10% chance to grant 200 CE to the capturing side.
🔮 Skills & Techniques

Skills are unlocked by defeating opponents and equipped in the Technique Archive. You can equip up to 9 skill slots (4 Special, 2 Ability, 1 Domain, 1 RCT, 1 Ultimate). During battle, click a skill button to activate it.

SPECIAL SLOTS (S1–S4)

Divergent Fist (20 CE) — Click an enemy pawn to destroy it and leave a cursed hazard on that square. Blocked by Infinity and Limitless.
Divine Dog (80 CE) — Summons a shikigami wolf (Knight + Bishop moves). Click again to retract. Cannot be re-summoned if destroyed.

Lapse Blue (60 CE) — Select any piece (not King), then click any square (not a back rank) to teleport it there instantly, capturing what's there. Blocked by active Infinity/Limitless (CE refunded if blocked).
Dismantle (200 CE) — Click an enemy piece; destroys all enemy pieces in a 3-wide horizontal sweep (col-1, col, col+1) on that row. Requires column line-of-sight.
Cursed Speech (80 CE) — Seals one random opponent skill for 20 moves. Once per battle only.
Copy (100 CE) — Copies one random skill from the opponent for a single use. Once per battle.
Nue (120 CE) — Summons the shikigami Nue (Bishop that can also teleport to any empty square). Cannot interact with King. Cannot be re-summoned if destroyed.
Heian Cleave (220 CE) — Auto-destroys the 2 most valuable enemy pieces simultaneously. No targeting needed.

Reverse Cursed Technique (80 CE) — Restores your most recently lost piece onto the board. Does not trigger Mahoraga adaptation.

DOMAIN SLOTS — 3-Tier System

Malevolent Shrine (500 CE) [Level 2] — Destroys 2 most valuable enemy pieces every 3 turns. Overpowers Level 1 domains. Clashes with Level 2. Collapses to Level 3.
Infinite Void (500 CE) [Level 2] — Seals enemy for 10 turns. Overpowers Level 1 domains. Clashes with Level 2. Collapses to Level 3. Mahoraga adapts after 6 turns.
True Mutual Love (500 CE) [Level 1] — Each turn a random skill fires automatically. Clashes with Level 1. Collapses to Level 2 or 3.
Self Embodiment of Perfection (500 CE) [Level 1] — Warps enemy pieces into pawns each turn. Clashes with Level 1. Collapses to Level 2 or 3.
Time Cell Moon Palace (500 CE) [Level 1] — Free +1 move per turn; enemy pieces that move are destroyed. Clashes with Level 1. Collapses to Level 2 or 3.
Chimera Shadow Garden (500 CE) [Level 1] — Shadow clones appear each turn for 10 turns. Clashes with Level 1. Collapses to Level 2 or 3.
Malevolent Shrine: Heian (800 CE) [Level 3] — Destroys 3 most valuable pieces every 2 turns. Overpowers ALL lower-level domains. Cannot be shattered.
🌐 Domain Expansion & Clashes

Domains have 3 power levels. When two domains meet:

  • Higher level vs lower level: The weaker domain collapses instantly — no clash, the stronger domain takes full control.
  • Same level vs same level: A Domain Clash occurs — both domains' effects are suppressed for 20 turns; only chess moves allowed.
  • Level 3 (Heian) overpowers everything — instant collapse. Level 2 (Void/Shrine) overpowers Level 1 instantly. Level 1 domains clash with each other.
  • When you expand a higher-level domain against a weaker one, the weaker collapses immediately — no waiting for next turn. The domain user enters burnout.
  • During a clash, check-win collapses the enemy domain and yours takes control.
  • Both domains' effects are suppressed for 20 turns during a clash.
  • Neither side can use any skill during a clash — only chess moves are allowed.
  • Check-win: if you put the enemy King in check during a clash, their domain collapses and yours takes over immediately.

Each domain clash has a unique visual: pink, gold, purple, crimson, depending on which domains are fighting.

☸ Mahoraga & Adaptation

Mahoraga is unique — it adapts to techniques used against it:

  • After blocking Infinity or Limitless 3 times, Mahoraga pierces through them permanently.
  • If an active domain is on the board while Mahoraga is present, Mahoraga's wheel spins each turn. After 3 turns, it collapses the domain entirely.
  • Destroying Mahoraga seals the Ten Shadows — it cannot be resummoned.
  • World Cutting Slash, Hollow Nuke, Hollow Purple, Fuga, and Ultimate Dark Flash can destroy Mahoraga regardless of adaptation.
∞ Infinity & Limitless

Infinity (active, 120 CE): blocks ALL enemy attacks — both chess captures and all attacking skills — until the start of your next turn.

Limitless (passive): automatically spends 50 CE to block each enemy attack. Makes all your pieces immune for the entire turn. Unlike Infinity, it can run out of CE.

Both are disabled during a Domain Clash. Lapse Blue is treated as an offensive action and is therefore blocked by active Infinity and Limitless (CE is refunded if blocked).

⚠ Infinity/Limitless Bypass Rules

No skill or attack can pierce Infinity or Limitless by default. The only ways to bypass them are:

  • A — Active domain: if the attacker has their own domain currently active, all their skills and attacks ignore Infinity/Limitless.
  • B — Mahoraga adapted: if Mahoraga has adapted to Infinity or Limitless, every skill and attack used by Mahoraga's owner permanently pierces through both barriers.
  • C — Domain Clash: during an active domain clash, Infinity and Limitless are fully suppressed for both sides.
  • D — Heavenly Restriction: a combatant with Heavenly Restriction (Zenin Toji and any player equipping that skill) ignores both barriers entirely.

If none of conditions A–D apply, Infinity and Limitless are absolute — no skill in the game can break them.

⚠ Exceptions: World Cutting Slash and Hollow Nuke

World Cutting Slash and Hollow Nuke are unblockable ultimate techniques that bypass ALL barriers including Infinity, Limitless, and even Mahoraga adaptation. They can destroy any piece on the board — King, Mahoraga, and Rika included. Their only counter is a higher-level domain or preventing the chant from completing.

⏱️ Domain Duration & Auto-Collapse

All domains now last a maximum of 20 turns. A countdown in the battle info panel shows remaining turns. When the timer reaches zero, the domain collapses automatically, triggering domain burnout (all skills + RCT disabled for 10 turns). Landing 2 Black Flashes skips this burnout.

Simple Domain lasts 8 turns and has a 5-turn cooldown after it ends. While active, it neutralizes ALL domain sure-hit effects — Malevolent Shrine, Cursed Existence infinite range, and all other domain offensive effects are completely suppressed.

🛡️ Infinity/Limitless & Active Domains

If you have an active domain, your attacks automatically bypass enemy Infinity and Limitless. The domain's power overrides these barriers. However, if the enemy also has an active domain, the higher-level domain wins — and if levels are equal, a Domain Clash occurs where both barriers are disabled.

⚡ Shinjuku Itadori (ELO 3200 MAX)

Yuji's kit is designed to counter Heian Sukuna:

  • Prince of Black Flash (passive) — 60% Black Flash chance on every attack. Starting at turn 10 (move 20), OPPI charges: automatically fires when opponent activates any domain — collapses it, blocks their domain slot for 10 turns, and skips their next turn. Recharges 20 turns after use.
  • Ultimate Dark Flash (600 CE, Ultimate) — Click a square adjacent to any friendly piece. Deals + shaped damage (target + 4 orthogonal neighbors). Soul damage can't be RCT'd for 10 turns. Grants +200 CE and reduces burnout by 2 turns.
  • Cursed Existence (600 CE, Level 1 domain) — 100% Black Flash chance, infinite skill range, all damage is permanent soul damage. Lasts 20 turns (domain auto-collapse). Collapses against Level 2+ domains.
  • Simple Domain (400 CE) — Blue barrier negates enemy domain sure-hit for 8 turns. Glass-shatter effect on end. 5-turn cooldown.
  • Win condition: Capture ALL of Yuji's pieces (not just the King). All 4 skills + RCT drop on defeat.
💚 CT Regeneration

CT Regeneration (60 CE) — A technique that accelerates burnout recovery. Each use reduces both RCT and Domain burnout by 1 turn (if currently in burnout). If not in burnout, it still counts toward your limit.

  • Has 30 uses total per battle. Track your uses in the battle info panel.
  • After 20 uses, your nose starts bleeding — a warning that you're approaching the brain's limit.
  • After 30 uses, your brain surpasses its limits: domain is permanently blocked for the rest of the battle, with a 10-turn burnout on top. References Mahito's words to Todo.
  • MAX characters drop CT Regeneration on defeat. All MAX bots (Gojo Strongest, Heian Sukuna, Shinjuku Itadori) can use it.
∞ Gojo Sensei — Simple Domain

Gojo Sensei now has Simple Domain in addition to his existing skills. This allows him to survive inside enemy domains by neutralizing the sure-hit effect. Unlock Simple Domain by defeating Gojo Sensei.

📜 Binding Vows

Before each battle you can choose a Binding Vow — a risk/reward pact that modifies the rules of combat:

  • Annihilation — all offensive skills enhanced (all offensive skills enhanced), but starting CE −200.
  • Speed — gain +1 extra chess move per turn, but all skill costs +50%.
  • Hunger — every piece you capture restores 100× its value in CE (massive snowball), but start with 0 CE.
  • Sacrifice — press X on any selected non-King piece to sacrifice it for 50× value CE (once per turn), but Black Flash is disabled.
  • Divine — starting CE ×3 and all cooldowns start at zero, but Ability slots A1 and A2 are permanently sealed.
  • Reversion — free automatic RCT every 2 turns restores a captured piece, but Domain slot is sealed.
⚔ Domain Clash Check-Win

When two domains clash, normal effects are suppressed and a 20-turn countdown begins. If either king is put in check during a clash, the side that delivered the check wins — the losing domain collapses instantly, and the winning domain takes full control of the board, resuming all its normal effects.

🩸 Body Integrity System

Every chess piece represents a part of the sorcerer's physical body. Taking damage has real consequences:

  • ♔ King = Brain — If captured, the sorcerer dies. Game over.
  • ♕ Queen = Heart — If the Queen is captured, all skill costs are doubled (×2 CE). After the Queen is recovered via RCT, the heart needs 10 turns to fully heal before costs normalize. Without a heart, the sorcerer fights at half power.
  • ♙ Queen-side pawns (A-D) = Left Arm — Both A and D file pawns must be captured to lose this arm. As long as at least 1 pawn remains on A-D files, the arm is functional.
  • ♙ King-side pawns (E-H) = Right Arm — Both E and H file pawns must be captured. Same rule as left arm.
  • ♖ Rooks, ♗ Bishops, ♘ Knights — These represent the torso, organs, and ribs. Losing them hurts (material disadvantage) but doesn't impose mechanical penalties.
🦾 Domain Expansion Arm Requirements

In the manga, domain expansion requires hand signs — both hands, except for the most powerful techniques:

  • Standard domains (Malevolent Shrine, True Mutual Love, SEP, TCMP, CSG, Cursed Existence) require both arms to activate. Lose one arm and these domains are sealed.
  • Infinite Void and Malevolent Shrine: Heian require only 1 arm. Gojo can cast Void with one hand (Six Eyes offloads motor control). Heian Sukuna has four arms (Imaginary Fierce God).
  • No arms = no domain at all.
  • All bots respect these restrictions — Sukuna can't expand Shrine if you capture his arm pawns!
🔥 RCT Burnout

Reverse Cursed Technique is powerful but has limits — just like in the manga, overusing it causes the technique to "burn out":

  • Each use of RCT tracks material restored: Pawns = 1pt, Knights/Bishops = 3pts, Rooks = 5pts, Queen = 9pts.
  • When 20 points of material have been restored, RCT enters a 10-turn burnout and cannot be used.
  • During burnout, the RCT slot is greyed out and a countdown is shown.
  • Skip the cooldown: Land 2 Black Flashes while in burnout to immediately recover!
  • This prevents infinite stalemates where both sides constantly resurrect pieces.
💥 Domain Collapse Burnout

When a domain collapses — whether overpowered, clash-ended, or time-expired — the sorcerer is left vulnerable:

  • All skills and RCT are disabled for 10 turns after a domain collapse.
  • This represents the massive cursed energy backlash of a domain failing.
  • Skip the cooldown: Land 2 Black Flashes to immediately recover all skills!
  • All domains now last a maximum of 20 turns before forced collapse.
⚡ Black Flash — Dramatic Reboot

Black Flash is no longer just a CE bonus — it's a dramatic comeback mechanic:

  • 10% chance on any damaging attack (disabled for Heavenly Restriction and Sacrifice vow).
  • Triggers a full-screen flash effect with dramatic "⚡ BLACK FLASH ⚡" title.
  • 2 consecutive Black Flashes while in burnout (RCT or Domain) immediately clears all burnouts.
  • This mirrors the manga: Sukuna and Gojo both used Black Flashes to recover from burnout mid-fight.
  • The battle info panel shows your Black Flash counter so you can track progress toward recovery.
⚔️ Fuga Rebalanced
  • Fuga is now a 6×6 area attack (up from 5×5) — even more devastating.
  • However, using Fuga immediately collapses your Heian Shrine domain and triggers domain burnout.
  • A high-risk, high-reward finisher — use it to end the fight, but be prepared to fight without skills for 10 turns.
